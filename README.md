# BlunderScope ♞

**Chess game review on your phone — like chess.com's Game Review, powered by Stockfish 19 running offline on the device.**

**مراجعة ماتشات الشطرنج على موبايلك — زي Game Review بتاع chess.com، بمحرك Stockfish 19 شغال على الموبايل نفسه من غير إنترنت.**

## ⬇️ Download / تحميل

Get the latest APK from **[Releases](https://github.com/Kamal142-D/blunderscope/releases/latest)**.

نزّل آخر نسخة من **[Releases](https://github.com/Kamal142-D/blunderscope/releases/latest)** وسطّبها (هتحتاج تسمح بتثبيت التطبيقات من مصادر غير معروفة).

Requires Android 8.0+ on a 64-bit (arm64) phone. The app checks this page for new versions and can update itself.

## ✨ Features / المميزات

- Every move classified: **Brilliant · Great · Best · Excellent · Good · Inaccuracy · Mistake · Miss · Blunder** — with the best move shown as an arrow
- Accuracy for both players, key moments, evaluation graph and eval bar
- Fetch your recent games from **chess.com** or **Lichess** by username, paste a PGN, open a `.pgn` file, or share a PGN to the app
- Several engines in parallel, and analysis keeps running in the background with a progress notification
- Arabic and English interface
- Your analyzed games are saved on the phone and reopen instantly
